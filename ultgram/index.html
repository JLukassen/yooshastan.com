01 / Built around choice

One messenger.
More ways to make it yours.

Ultgram is the public identity for the Yooshastan ULT Messenger project. It combines ideas from favorite alternative Telegram clients through focused, individually tested enhancements.

03 / Practical privacy, clear limits

Control without the mystique.

Two Android build paths

A Google-free build is the first testing target, with a Firebase-compatible option planned. Optional microG support is not a requirement for every user.

Know what a proxy does

Proxies can change the route to Telegram, but they do not make a Telegram account anonymous to Telegram. Ultgram will not add Yooshastan advertising or tracking.

Client-side conveniences

Local appearance and layout extras are planned without promising paid Telegram Premium capabilities or bypassing server-side entitlements.

Hybrid RCS companion

SMS/MMS management and Google Messages RCS handoff are exploratory future work. Third-party full RCS chat access is not guaranteed.
